DOCS.

HOODFACTORY is an AI-agent-driven token launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Chat to launch a token, look one up, check your wallet, swap, and send — every transaction is signed by your own wallet. This page explains how the agent, the launchpad, and the on-chain mechanics work.

OVERVIEW

An AI agent that actually does things on-chain

Instead of menus and forms, HOODFACTORY gives you one chat box. Describe what you want in plain language and the agent prepares the transaction; your wallet signs it and pays the network gas. HOODFACTORY never holds your funds, keys, or private data.

WHAT THE AGENT CAN DO

  • Create a token on the launchpad
  • Check your ETH and token balances
  • Swap ETH ↔ token or token ↔ token
  • Send ETH or tokens to any address

WHO SIGNS & PAYS

The agent only prepares an action and shows a confirmation card. Nothing happens until you approve it in your own wallet. You pay only the Robinhood Chain gas fee — HOODFACTORY charges no creation fee.

CHAT AGENT

The HOODFACTORY agent

The built-in agent is free to try — 10 messages per wallet every 24 hours, no setup and no API key required. Usage is tracked per wallet address, so it stays consistent even if you disconnect and reconnect.

FREE TIER

10 messages per wallet / 24h. When the limit is reached, the agent lets you keep going in one of two ways below.

KEEP GOING

  • Unlimited — $10 / month, paid on-chain in ETH to the protocol treasury. Access unlocks for 30 days.
  • Bring your own key (BYOK) — build a custom agent with your own API key (see “Own Agent” below).

The agent prepares transactions only. It never moves funds on its own, and it will always ask you to confirm and sign in your wallet. When sending, it requires the full destination address and never guesses it.

TOKEN LAUNCHPAD

How a token launch works

Every token created through HOODFACTORY is a fixed-supply ERC-20 with an instant, permanently locked market. There is no bonding curve and no migration step — trading is live in the same pool from the moment of creation.

1 · MINT

Fixed supply

1,000,000,000 tokens are minted. The token has no owner, no mint, no pause, and no blacklist.

2 · ALLOCATION

20% configured allocation

200,000,000 (20%) are transferred to the allocation wallet configured in the launchpad.

3 · LIQUIDITY

80% locked LP

800,000,000 (80%) are deposited as single-sided Token/WETH liquidity on Uniswap V3.

4 · LOCK

Permanent

The LP position (NFT) is owned and permanently locked by the launchpad — the principal cannot be withdrawn.

FEES

Trading & LP fees

Swaps through HOODFACTORY carry the underlying Uniswap V3 pool fee plus a small platform fee. Liquidity-provider fees earned by the locked position are split between the token creator and the protocol.

Total (launchpad tokens)~1.5%Excludes price impact, slippage & gas

LP fee — protocol share45%Of collected pool fees

External tokens swapped in the Swap section use their own standard Uniswap V3 pool fee (0.05%–1% depending on the pool). A default slippage guard is applied to every swap.

MARKET & SWAP

Trade launchpad tokens and beyond

MARKET

Every token created through HOODFACTORY is listed automatically. You can buy and sell straight from the card — orders route through the HOODFACTORY router into each token's locked Uniswap V3 pool. The Trending row ranks tokens by 24-hour swap volume.

SWAP

The Swap section is not limited to HOODFACTORY tokens — any token with a Uniswap V3 pool on Robinhood Chain can be swapped. Pick from the list or paste a contract address; routing goes through a direct pool or via WETH when needed. Always do your own research on external tokens.

OWN AGENT — BYOK

Build a custom agent with your own key

Prefer no limits and full control? Assemble your own agent and connect your own API key. The call goes straight from your browser to the provider you choose — your key is never sent to HOODFACTORY's servers, and provider usage is billed to your own account.

PROVIDERS & TOOLS

  • Choose Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google and paste your key
  • Give the agent a goal: Research & Analysis, or On-chain Tools
  • Optional Whale Radar tools for on-chain signal, holders, transfers & risk checks

SAME ON-CHAIN POWERS

A BYOK agent can do everything the built-in agent can — create tokens, check balances, swap and send — all built on the HOODFACTORY contracts, and always signed by your own wallet. Your saved agents live in your browser, per wallet address; your API key is never stored.

DISCLAIMER

Important notes

HOODFACTORY is experimental software currently running on a testnet. Nothing here is financial, investment, or security advice. Creating a token does not give it any value. On-chain actions are irreversible and are always signed by your own wallet — verify every confirmation card, contract address, and destination address before approving. Any risk signals shown in the app are basic heuristics from public data, not a smart-contract audit. Always do your own research.
